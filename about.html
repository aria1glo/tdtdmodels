About the agency

“Never be afraid to be bold. Never be afraid to be yourself. We need individuality in a world of people who want to be the same.”

Kamani Karseladze, Founder

TDTD Models is a New York based agency founded in 2024 by Kamani Karseladze, with the aim of curating and developing models into attractable talent for clientele across the country. The agency boasts and takes pride in its diverse talent roster, while remaining unyielding to the highest standards of excellence needed to succeed in the fashion industry.
